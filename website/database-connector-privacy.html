Privacy notice

How Database Connector works, what it processes, and the choices available to you.

Product overviewPrivacy noticeService terms

Effective October 9, 2026. A product policy from Nektron, Inc.

Database Connector is operated by Nektron, Inc. This notice describes the information processed when you connect a database, manage that connection on Nektron, and use the connector through ChatGPT.

Information processed and why

  • Account information: your Nektron account identifier, verified-email status, membership and access permissions link your connections to your account. The NektronAI signup service separately collects the profile and login information described in the company policy. Connector responses may include an account reference used to ensure management links open for the correct signed-in account.
  • Connection information: the display name, engine, host, port, database or service name, schema where applicable, permitted tables or collections and connection status let the service locate and restrict access to your database. You enter a database username and password on Nektron's secure setup page. Credentials are stored encrypted using AWS Secrets Manager and used by the database execution service; they are not included in MCP tool results.
  • Queries and database results: the connector receives the SQL and parameters, or MongoDB collection and aggregation pipeline, together with the connection identifier selected for your request. It reads authorized database schemas, collection metadata and records to answer that request and returns bounded results to ChatGPT. Results can contain personal information present in the selected data. Connect only data you are authorized to share. Do not connect tables or collections containing authentication secrets, payment-card data, protected health information or government identifiers for use through this connector.
  • Operational records: we record account/connection references, operation, status, time, duration, row/result size and a query fingerprint for reliability, access enforcement and investigating failures or abuse. Connector audit records do not contain raw SQL, parameter values, MongoDB pipelines, passwords or result rows. The connector does not keep a persistent query-results archive; results pass through the service to the requesting client.
  • Support and billing: messages you send to support are used to resolve your request. If you purchase a plan through the independently visited Nektron website, billing identifiers, subscription status and provider events are used to maintain your plan. Stripe handles payment forms; database credentials and query results are not sent to Stripe for payment processing.

Recipients

AWS provides application hosting, encrypted credential storage, configuration and operational storage. The configured database host receives the authenticated queries needed for your request. ChatGPT receives requested query results and schema or connection metadata; its own privacy controls apply to information in your conversations. Stripe receives information necessary for independently initiated billing. Nektron personnel may process information needed for support and operation. We do not sell your personal information. The company policy describes legal and security-related disclosures.

Retention and removal

Connection configuration and encrypted credentials remain while the connection is saved. Selecting Remove revokes new connector access through that saved connection and removes its active configuration. A query already running may finish. Eligible service-managed credentials are scheduled for deletion with a seven-day recovery window. Imported or shared credentials may be retained; the connector does not delete database users or change passwords on the source database. A failed cleanup is reported as pending and can be retried.

Removal retains a minimal receipt containing the connection reference, display name, requesting account reference, time, revision and credential-cleanup status. The receipt does not contain the password. Completed cleanup receipts become eligible for deletion after 30 days; unresolved cleanup records remain until the issue is reconciled, then their 30-day period starts. Retry operations do not extend that period. Setup receipts needed for safe retry and account/entitlement records remain while the connection or connector account is active. A verified connector closure request is handled through support, with a target of removing eligible connector-only account records within 30 days, or sooner where applicable law requires. We explain any lawful exception or extension.

Connector audit records have a 30-day expiration setting and configured connector service logs have 30-day retention. Expiration cleanup is asynchronous, rather than an exact-second deletion guarantee. Connector OAuth grant families and their hashed credentials become eligible for cleanup 30 days after the grant family expires. Bounded housekeeping runs during normal authorization activity; it preserves active grants and credentials belonging to other clients. Service inactivity or cleanup failures can delay housekeeping. Verified account requests can also be handled by an operator rather than waiting for routine housekeeping.

Routine connector support cases are reviewed for deletion 12 months after closure. Necessary accounting records for paid purchases, when offered, are retained under Nektron's internal seven-year financial-record schedule, subject to applicable legal requirements and documented holds. This does not justify retaining database passwords or query results with billing records. We retain only the information needed for the relevant purpose and explain exceptions on request.

The connector registry currently has a 35-day point-in-time recovery window. Recoverable copies can temporarily contain older records after active deletion. Erasure records must be reapplied before a restored copy is returned to service. Any other backup or export containing scoped account information is identified when handling a deletion request; it is isolated from routine use and handled without deleting unrelated products' data. We do not control your source database backups or copies held by ChatGPT.

Removing a connection does not delete your actual database or information already returned to ChatGPT. Manage those copies through your database and ChatGPT controls.

Your controls and contact

Choose a restricted read-only database account, limit its table or collection permissions, remove a saved connection on Nektron, disconnect the app in ChatGPT, or revoke its source database permissions. Database credentials should never be pasted into chat or sent to support. Contact info@nektron.ai for access, correction, account deletion or privacy requests. Nektron may need to verify the requester's authority before acting.

Requests, location and updates

Nektron operates from the United States and uses AWS infrastructure in the United States for this connector. ChatGPT, Stripe and your database provider have their own processing locations and privacy practices. Contact us about international processing or safeguards before providing data that requires a specific hosting arrangement. This service is not offered as a regulated-data hosting product.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing or obtain a copy of your information, and to raise a concern with a data-protection authority. We assess requests under the law that applies to you and do not require a database password to verify a privacy request. Closing Database Connector alone does not erase the shared NektronAI account or records needed by other Nektron products. Tell us explicitly if you also want to close your shared account so that the broader request can be reviewed.

We use account and connection information to provide the requested service, operational information to secure and maintain it, and necessary records to meet legal obligations. Where an activity requires consent, it must be obtained before that activity. We update this notice when these practices change and identify the effective date on the published page.
