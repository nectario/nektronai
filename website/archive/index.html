GrowNet

GrowNet is a new kind of AI architecture that starts small and grows only when it needs to. Instead of training one massive, fixed model, it learns locally and adds capacity under clear, deterministic rules.

  • Efficient growth: add capacity only under strict, measurable triggers
  • Interpretability: spatial wiring and growth decisions are inspectable
  • Reproducibility: deterministic guardrails and cross-language parity for experiments
  • Designed for research and real-world systems, not just benchmarks
