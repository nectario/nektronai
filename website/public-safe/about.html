Company view

Why NektronAI exists

NektronAI was created to explore the possibility that progress in AI may require more than scaling current methods. Selected research materials are currently private while the company prepares the right disclosure path.

That research ambition is paired with practical company building. Products provide revenue, pressure, and evidence, keeping the company shaped by both long-horizon thinking and real-world constraints.

Founder / Research Lead

Nektarios Kalogridis

Founder, Research Lead, and CEO of NektronAI, with a background spanning more than two decades of Wall Street systems engineering, AI model building, and long-horizon product development.

Role Founder / Research Lead / CEO

Nektarios Kalogridis founded NektronAI to pursue a deeper question in AI: whether meaningful progress may require new learning architectures rather than only larger versions of today's prevailing training assumptions. He brings to that effort a combination of hands-on AI model building and production-grade systems engineering.

Across more than twenty years in financial technology, he has built low-latency trading and execution systems for major investment banks in environments where correctness, throughput, and response time are business-critical.

He has also spent years building AI models and products, including DeepTrading.ai and research-led systems spanning forecasting, backtesting, automation, and AI-assisted workflows.

Focus AI foundations, model design, and adaptive learning systems
Background AI-driven systems, Wall Street low-latency trading, and eTrading platforms
Current work DeepTrading.ai and research-led product development
