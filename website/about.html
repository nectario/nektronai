About Us

NektronAI is an umbrella AI startup spanning research, products, and services. Our centerpiece research program, GrowNet, explores neuron-centric AI where learning is local and event-driven, and capacity grows when novelty demands it.

Alongside research, we build practical products and deliver AI services to help teams ship working systems. The thesis is straightforward: better foundations create better applications, and real applications create the pressure needed to build better foundations.

Leadership

Nektarios Kalogridis is the Founder and CEO of NektronAI, where he builds at the intersection of research ambition and production execution. Over a 20+ year career, he has engineered low-latency trading and data platforms for major investment banks, specializing in systems where correctness, resilience, and speed are non-negotiable. At NektronAI, he leads GrowNet, a growth-based neural architecture exploring new foundations for learning and scalability, and drives applied products including DeepTrading.ai, InterviewHelperAI, and TagMySpend—all designed to pressure-test ideas in real workflows and feed those lessons back into research.
