About Us

NektronAI (Nektron, Inc.) combines GrowNet research, applied products, and selective AI services.

What we do

NektronAI spans research, products, and services. Our centerpiece research program, GrowNet, explores neuron-centric AI where learning is local and event-driven, and capacity grows when novelty demands it.

Alongside research, we build practical products and deliver AI services to help teams ship working systems. The thesis is straightforward: better foundations create better applications, and real applications create the pressure needed to build better foundations.

Leadership

Nektarios Kalogridis is the Founder and CEO of NektronAI, building at the intersection of research ambition and production execution.

Over a 20+ year career, he has engineered low-latency trading and data platforms—systems where correctness, resilience, and speed are non-negotiable.

At NektronAI, he leads GrowNet (a growth-based neural architecture exploring local, event-driven learning) and oversees applied products including DeepTrading.ai, InterviewHelperAI, and TagMySpend.
