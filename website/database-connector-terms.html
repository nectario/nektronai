How Database Connector works, what it processes, and the choices available to you.

Effective October 9, 2026. A product policy from Nektron, Inc.

The service

Database Connector by Nektron, Inc. lets you explore databases you are authorized to access using read-only SQL or MongoDB aggregation pipelines through ChatGPT. Supported profiles are MySQL 8.4.5, PostgreSQL 17, SQL Server 2022, Oracle 19 and Oracle Free 26ai (server major 23), and MongoDB 8. Supported operations and limits vary by engine. You need a Nektron account, an appropriate ChatGPT account, and a database reachable through a supported, secure network path. Nektron does not make a private database publicly accessible.

Your responsibilities

Use a dedicated, restricted read-only database account. You are responsible for authorization to connect the database and share the requested results with ChatGPT, for your chosen database grants, and for protecting your account. Do not send database passwords or connection strings in chat. Enter credentials only in the secure setup form. Do not use the connector to access other customers' data, circumvent restrictions, or process data prohibited by the platform's policies.

Query behavior and limitations

Source queries are read-only; the service records operational audit metadata and temporary concurrency records. Relational requests are subject to schema/table and SQL-function policies. MongoDB requests use a bounded aggregation pipeline against one permitted collection and reject JavaScript, cross-collection lookup, output, and merge stages. All requests are subject to execution-time, concurrency, row-count and response-size limits. Results may be truncated, incomplete or affected by source data quality. Separate queries do not share a database snapshot. Check generated interpretations against your source data before relying on them for consequential decisions. No unlimited usage or fixed uptime guarantee is offered.

Plans and payments

Free provides one saved connection. When Pro is made available, its listed price is USD $12 per month for up to five saved connections total, not five additional connections. Disabled saved configurations also count toward the limit. Any taxes, renewal terms and cancellation/refund conditions must be disclosed before purchase. Purchases take place on the independently visited Nektron website through Stripe; the ChatGPT connector does not collect payment details or initiate a digital subscription checkout.

Live payments and public enrollment are currently disabled. A provider test-mode transaction does not create a real charge or a live subscription. Paid subscriptions are not offered through this private preview. Any paid service must present its renewal, cancellation, refund and tax terms before a customer is asked to purchase.

When a paid entitlement ends, the first saved connection remains available under Free; additional saved connections become inaccessible without deleting the actual databases. Removing a connection frees its saved slot. Removal is a separate confirmed management action, and reconnecting requires setup again.

Removing access and support

Removing a saved connection stops new connector queries through it. It does not delete source data, revoke the source database user, close the Nektron account or delete information already returned to ChatGPT. The product privacy notice explains credential cleanup and retained records. Disconnecting in ChatGPT and removing a saved database configuration are separate controls.

Contact info@nektron.ai for service, billing or account assistance. Nektron may restrict access necessary to address misuse, compromised credentials or a service failure. The existing company terms govern general website content and limitations to the extent applicable; these service terms do not limit rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Privacy requests and service changes

The product privacy notice explains retention and support-managed connector closure. A request to close Database Connector is distinct from deleting your shared NektronAI account. We verify the requester's authority and explain retained records or applicable exceptions. For questions about account access or service changes, contact info@nektron.ai.
